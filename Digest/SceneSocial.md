# Social scene

Computing's pioneers posting as if they had phones: a feed of posts, alerts you swipe to triage, profiles you can follow, and compose and search sheets, all on one branded backdrop. You are Ada Lovelace (`@countess`). Its entry is `socialScene` ("Social", "A feed, alerts, and profiles", `ic_group`), first in `ShowcasePages.entries`. The Feed and the Alerts are the scene's two roots; each pushes its post, profile and alerts panels on its own back stack. The shared scene rules are in `DemoScenes.md`.

## The root — `SocialScene()`

- `rememberCoroutineScope()`, then the model: `rememberSaveable(saver = SocialModel.saver(scope)) { SocialModel(scope) }`. A `LaunchedEffect` runs the first load; a `DisposableEffect` cancels pending work as the scene leaves. `showsRemote` is its own saved flag. `SocialPostActions` is remembered with the scene's `LocalClipboard`: Copy puts the post's text on the clipboard as a `ClipEntry` and the system confirms the copy, so the scene shows no toast of its own.
- Everything sits in `SocialBackdrop`, around the roots form of `AMScene` (`containerColor = Transparent`): `current = model.currentView`, `landing = Feed`, `backStack = model.stack(view)`, and the remote in `floatingActions` (`AMFloatingActions(contentDescription = "Actions")`). Coming back to the Feed by back cancels pending work like any other move.
- **Each root** is `SocialRootStack`: a Navigation 3 `NavDisplay` over that root's `SnapshotStateList<SocialRoute>`, whose `Root` entry draws `SocialFeed` or `SocialAlerts`, and whose panels draw `SocialPostDetail`, `SocialProfile` and `SocialAlertsPanel`; back and each panel's arrow call `model.pop(view)`. Beside it, the same root composes `SocialOverlays`, so the overlays sit inside the scene's hosts and registry (only the showing root is composed, so there's always one copy).
- **Overlays** (`SocialOverlays`): the compose `AMSheet` (titled `composeTitle`, "New post" or "Reply") and the search `AMSheet` ("Search"), both full height on `socialSheetSurface()`; the "Delete post?" `AMAlert` while `confirmDeleteId` is set ("It's removed from your profile and the feed.", Cancel and a destructive Delete); the post menu (`SocialPostMenu`); `AMBanner(banner)`, whose tap calls `undoMute()`; `AMToast(toast)`. No sheet carries the discard guard.

### The look

- **`SocialBackdrop`**: the one background behind every screen, so the scene always looks like itself: an `AMBackdrop` whose art is a linear gradient from the top start corner to the bottom end corner, a light social-network blue `#A8D6FF` into a deeper `#6BA8FA` in light mode, slate ink `#1C2433` into a lighter slate `#2A3446` in dark mode. The scene root sits on it, and each panel draws its own (`AMPanel` on a transparent container), so a push slides an opaque screen and frosted surfaces always have art to blur. Text on it is the scheme's `onSurface`; section headers over it take their over-art look.
- **`socialFill()`**: the page surfaces (cards and the profile's tabs) are `Solid` in light mode and `Frosted` in dark mode; `Modifier.socialCard(padding)` is `amCard` in that fill. **`socialSheetSurface()`**: the sheets follow, `Primary` in light mode and `Frosted` in dark mode; what they hold is solid (a sheet's fresh context).
- **`SocialFabClearance`** = 88 dp, the room every scrolling view leaves at its end.

## The model — `SocialModel`

A plain `@Stable` class of Compose state, built on the scene's coroutine scope.

- **Data**: `users` (the samples), `posts` (newest first) and `replies` as snapshot lists, `notifications`, and sets for what you've done: `liked`, `reposted`, `followed`, `muted`, `readAlerts` (seeded with the alerts that start read). A post's counts are its own plus yours (`likes(post)`, `reposts(post)`); a follower count adds you while you follow (`followerCount`). `isLoading` (true until the first load lands), `isRefreshing`.
- **Navigation**: `currentView: SocialView` (`Feed`, `Alerts`); `feedStack` and `alertsStack`, each starting with `SocialRoute.Root`; `stack(view)`. Panels are `SocialRoute.Post(postId, serial)`, `Profile(userId, serial)` and `Alerts(serial)`: the serial, taken from a counter, keeps every pushed entry its own, so the same post can be pushed twice on one stack. `alertFilter: SocialAlertFilter` (`All`, `Mentions`).
- **Overlays**: `postMenuId` (the post in the spotlight), `composing` and `composeMode` (`SocialComposeMode.Post` or `Reply(postId)`, set as the sheet opens and never cleared, so the title holds while it slides away), `searching`, `searchQuery` (a `TextFieldState`), `searchTopics`, `confirmDeleteId`, `banner`, `toast`.
- **Derived**: `feed` (posts by people you haven't muted), `unreadCount` (the bell), `shownNotifications` (under the filter; alerts from muted people and about deleted posts drop out), `replies(postId)`, `replyCount`, `posts(userId)`, `searchResults`, `isSearchIdle`, `trimmedQuery`, `composeTitle`, `replyTarget`, `postMenuPost`, `selfUser`, `user(id)`, `post(id)`.
- **Requests are stand-ins.** `load()` (the scene's `LaunchedEffect`) waits 1.2 s, then shows the posts. `refresh()` (pulling the feed down) is a `Job` in the scene's scope: after 1 s it puts the next refresh-pool post on top and toasts "Feed updated", and once the pool is spent toasts "No new posts". `cancelPendingWork()` cancels it and clears the indicator; it runs on every push, root swap, panel replacement, back to the Feed, Exit, and as the scene leaves, so its toast never lands on another screen.
- **Navigation actions**: `openPost`, `openProfile` and `openAlertsPanel()` (the bell) push onto the showing root's stack, unless the same panel (same post, person, or the alerts, whatever its serial) is already on top, so a double tap opens one panel; `pop(view)`; `switchTo(view)` swaps the root inside `amInstantSwitch`; `openOwnProfile()` leaves the showing root with only your profile over it (nothing changes when that's already so); `openSearchResult(id)` closes the search sheet and leaves the showing root with only that post over it; `open(notification)` marks it read and pushes the profile for a follow, otherwise its post while it exists.
- **Rules it keeps.** **Mute is the person**: `mute(userId)` (from the post menu or an alert's swipe) hides their posts, alerts and search results and shows the Info banner "Muted @aturing" / "Their posts and alerts are hidden. Tap to undo."; `undoMute()` brings them back and toasts "Unmuted @aturing". `confirmDelete(id)` raises the alert; `delete(id)` removes the post, its replies (its alerts drop out with it) and any panel showing it from both stacks, and toasts "Post deleted". `toggleLike`, `toggleRepost`, `toggleRead`, `toggleFollow` ("Following @handle" / "Unfollowed @handle"). `beginPost()`, `beginReply(postId)`, `send(text)` (the trimmed text: a post on top of the feed at "now", toast "Posted", or a reply at "now", toast "Reply sent"; it closes the sheet). `beginSearch()` empties the query and the topics as the sheet opens.
- **Search** matches the trimmed query in a post's text or its author's name or handle, ignoring case, among posts by people you haven't muted, inside the chosen topics when any are chosen; topics alone are a search.
- **Saved** (`SocialModel.saver(scope)`, a `mapSaver`): the posts as ids in feed order (with the text of those you composed), the replies as ids (with yours), every set, whether it's still loading, the current view, both stacks (routes as strings), the filter, the serial and composed counters, the post menu, the compose sheet and its mode, the search sheet, its query and topics, and the delete alert. A refresh in flight isn't kept.

## Samples — `SocialSamples`

Ten people with Wikimedia Commons portraits (`commonsPhoto`): Ada Lovelace (you), Grace Hopper, Claude Shannon, Alan Turing, Margaret Hamilton, Katherine Johnson, John von Neumann, Barbara Liskov, Donald Knuth, Dennis Ritchie, each with a bio, join date, place, website and counts. Nine posts, newest first (Liskov 15m, Johnson 27m, Shannon 41m, Ada 2h, Hopper 3h with the logbook moth, Hamilton 5h beside the printed Apollo code at 4:5, Turing 12h, von Neumann 1d, Ada's Note G 4d), every topic (Engines, Bugs, Information, Chess, Space, Languages) on at least one. Twelve replies. Ten alerts, the four newest unread (the bell reads 4), four of them mentions or replies. A refresh pool of three posts (Hopper, Knuth, Shannon).

## The surfaces

- **Feed** (`SocialFeed`, the landing view): a `Scaffold` on a transparent container under `AMPageHeader("Feed")`, collapsing as it scrolls, whose actions are `SceneAppearanceButton()` (solid over the backdrop) and the bell (`AMIconButton` "Alerts" with an `AMBadge` of `unreadCount`, pushing the alerts panel, so back returns to the feed). Under it, Material's `PullToRefreshBox` around a `LazyColumn` of post cards in the gutter, `spacing.md` apart, ending `SocialFabClearance` above the navigation bar. While the first load runs, each post shows as a placeholder card (`AMSkeleton` circle, a line and two to four lines of text, a short line for the counts) and the list doesn't scroll; the header stays live.
- **Post card** (`SocialPostCard`): `socialCard()`, clickable ("Open post") and `amHoldable` (the post menu, with Copy and Delete or Mute as TalkBack custom actions). The author's avatar (`SocialAvatarButton`, "Ada Lovelace profile", opening the profile) beside `SocialIdentityHeader` and `SocialPostBody`, then `SocialPostCounts` (Reply opens the post) with ⋮ ("More options", the post menu), inset so its first glyph lines up under the text.
- **`SocialIdentityHeader(author, timestamp, stacked)`**: the name (`titleMedium`), handle and time (`bodyMedium`, `onSurfaceVariant`) on one line, the time at the end, when that line fits (its intrinsic width against the room); otherwise, and always when `stacked`, the name over "handle · time", each line ellipsized. TalkBack reads one line of text, "name, handle, time".
- **`SocialPostBody(post)`**: the text in `bodyLarge`, then the photo (`AMRemoteImage`, labeled with its description) filling the width at its aspect ratio in `shapes.medium`.
- **`SocialPostCounts`**: an `AMCardActionRow` of Reply (its reply count), Repost ("Repost" / "Reposted", active in `AMSemantic.Success`) and Like (heart / filled heart, "Like" / "Liked", active in `AMSemantic.Destructive`), and an optional trailing slot.
- **Post menu** (`SocialPostMenu`): `AMSpotlight` over `postMenuPost`: a still copy of the card (avatar, identity, text and photo, padded `spacing.lg`) over one `AMActionGridRow`: Copy and Delete (destructive, asks first) on your own post, Copy and Mute on anyone else's. Each tile closes the menu.
- **Post panel** (`SocialPostDetail`): `AMPanel("Post")` on its own `SocialBackdrop`: a card with the author (avatar button and the stacked identity), the text and photo, the topic as a neutral `AMTag`, and `SocialPostCounts` (Reply opens the compose sheet in reply mode); then `AMSection("Replies", Rows)` in the scene's fill with a bare `AMListRow` per reply (the avatar button, name and time, the text), or with none an `AMSectionHeader("Replies")` over "No replies yet." in `bodySmall`. The post is looked up live; a deleted post draws nothing while its panel leaves.
- **Profile panel** (`SocialProfile`): `AMPanel(user.name)` on its own backdrop: a header card padded `spacing.lg`, centered (a `Large` avatar hidden from TalkBack, the name in `titleLarge` as a heading, the handle, the bio, Followers / Following / Posts each read as one element such as "842 Followers", and for anyone but you a full-width Follow (Primary) or Following (Secondary)); then `AMTabs` in the scene's fill, Posts and About (the choice saved): Posts is a card of `SocialPostRow`s without the author (the text over "2h · Engines") that push the post, or `AMEmptyState` "No posts yet" ("Posts you share show up here." / "When Grace posts, it shows up here."); About is a card of `AMLabeledContent` Joined, Location, Website.
- **Alerts** (`SocialAlerts`, the other root; `SocialAlertsPanel`, the bell's panel): as the root, a `Scaffold` under `AMPageHeader("Alerts")`; as a panel, `AMPanel("Alerts")` on its own backdrop. Either holds a full-width `AMSegmentedControl` (All, Mentions) over a card of alert rows, or `AMEmptyState` on the backdrop ("No alerts" / "Likes, replies, and new followers show up here."; "No mentions" / "When someone mentions or replies to you, it shows up here.").
- **Alert row** (`SocialAlertRow`): an `AMSwipeableRow` around a tappable `AMListRow` (`open`): the sender's `Small` avatar with an Info `AMBadgedBox` dot while unread, "Grace Hopper liked your post" (semibold while unread) over the time and the quoted words (two lines), and the kind's glyph at the end; "Unread" as its state for TalkBack. A full swipe toward the start marks it read or unread (Info, `mark_email_read` / `mark_email_unread`, the row settles back); toward the end it mutes the sender (the neutral gray band, `tint = null`, `volume_off`), which takes the row away with all their alerts. TalkBack offers both as actions on the row.
- **Compose sheet** (`SocialComposeSheet`): "Replying to @cshannon" (the post's author) in reply mode; a card padded `spacing.lg` with your `Small` avatar beside an `AMTextEditor` ("Write a post" / "Write a reply", five lines growing to twelve, focused as the sheet opens with the caret at the end); then, at the sheet's bottom, a "12/280" count (`bodySmall`, tabular figures, red past 280) and a full-width Post or Reply, disabled while the text is blank or too long. The draft lives in the sheet, so any close but sending drops it.
- **Search sheet** (`SocialSearchSheet`): an `AMSearchField` ("Search posts and people", focused with the caret at the end) in a card padded `spacing.lg`, a many-choice `AMChipGroup` of the topics, then the results: `AMEmptyState` "Search posts" ("Find posts by what they say, who wrote them, or their topic.") while idle, "No matches" ("No posts match “query”." / "No posts in these topics yet.") when nothing matches, otherwise a scrolling card of `SocialPostRow`s with their authors, each closing the sheet and opening its post over the showing root.

## The remote

Compose (`ic_edit_square`), Search (`ic_search`) over Feed (`ic_feed`), Alerts (`ic_notifications`), Profile (`ic_account_circle`), Exit (`ic_logout`), the same wherever the scene stands. Feed and Alerts are view tiles: the showing one is `active`; a tap closes the menu, then `switchTo` swaps the root inside `amInstantSwitch`, each coming back as it was left. Profile closes the menu and leaves the showing root with only your own profile over it. Compose and Search close the menu and open their sheets. Exit closes the menu, cancels pending work and calls `LocalCatalogNavigator.current.back()`.

## Reaching each screen

From the Feed (the landing view): a card → its post panel (Reply → the Reply sheet); an avatar → that profile; ⋮ or a press and hold on a card → the post menu (on Ada's own posts, Delete → the alert); pull down → refresh; the bell → the alerts panel. The remote: Compose → the New post sheet; Search → the search sheet; Alerts → the Alerts root (swipe a row either way); Profile → Ada's profile; Feed → back to the Feed. Back walks panels, then swaps Alerts to the Feed, then leaves the scene.
