# Music scene

The owner's jmsdnns.com catalog, streamed: covers read from the MP3s' tags, the queue, the Player and its controls card, on art backdrops, art rows and frosted surfaces. Its entry is `musicScene` ("Music", "Artists, albums and a player over the art", `ic_play_circle`), second in `ShowcasePages.entries`. The shared scene rules are in `DemoScenes.md`.

## The root — `MusicScene()`

- `rememberCoroutineScope()` and the application context, then the model: `rememberSaveable(saver = MusicModel.saver(scope, context)) { MusicModel(scope, context) }`. A `LaunchedEffect` runs `load()`; a `DisposableEffect` calls `stop()` as the scene leaves the composition, so playback stops and the audio focus goes with it.
- `AMScene`'s roots form: `current = model.rootView`, `landing = Artists`, `onCurrentChange` setting the root view, `backStack` the Artists stack for Artists and none for the Player; its remote in `floatingActions`: `AMFloatingActions(showingMenu, contentDescription = "Actions") { MusicMenu }`; and `overlays = { MusicOverlays(model) }` (the spotlight and the toast), composed beside the roots, so a switch of root (Play from the spotlight lands on the Player) never cuts them off mid-fade.
- **Roots.** `Artists` is `MusicArtistsStack`: a Navigation 3 `NavDisplay` over the model's `backStack` (`MusicRoute.Artists` → `MusicArtistsView`, `Artist(id)` → `MusicArtistPage`, `Album(id)` → `MusicAlbumPage`; `onBack` pops), on `AMTheme.Surface.art` (the library's dark stand-in for art, showing while Navigation 3 moves between pages). Each page is its own backdrop (the Artists root and the artist page on their art rows, `AMArtRowsBackdrop`; the album page on its cover's art), so on every page the light/dark chip and the toolbar buttons are solid, section headers take their over-art look, and frosted surfaces blur that page's own art. `Player` is `MusicPlayerView`, outside the stack, so a trip to the Player leaves the stack and every page in it as they were: Artists → American Food → Street Fight, tap a track, the Player shows, then the Artists tile lands on Street Fight where it was scrolled, and back walks up to the artist page.
- **Back**: the spotlight or the remote, then the Queue sheet; on Artists, the stack's panels, then out to the catalog; on the Player, an instant swap to Artists, the stack as it was.
- **Status bar icons**: the art is dark whatever the appearance, so each view over it holds the library's claim on light icons (`AMLightStatusBarIcons`, counted per window, `Navigation.md`): the Player always (`AMLightStatusBarIcons()`), the Artists root while its header lies on the art (`AMLightStatusBarIcons(enabled = overArt)`), and the artist and album pages through `AMArtPanelChrome` while its bar is at rest. Claims overlap and end in any order as Navigation 3 brings pages in and out and the remote swaps roots; the window takes the appearance's icons back only when the last ends (a collapsed header, a scrolled panel bar, leaving the scene), so a light/dark flip on a panel, then a pop, leaves the root's own icons.
- **Exit** (the remote's last tile) closes the menu, calls `stop()` and `LocalCatalogNavigator.current.back()`.

## The model — `MusicModel`

A plain `@Stable` class of Compose state on the scene's coroutine scope; the scene's one source of truth.

- **Types**: `MusicRootView` (`Artists`, `Player`), `MusicRoute` (`Artists`, `Artist(id)`, `Album(id)`), `MusicRepeat` (`Off`, `All`, `One`; `next` cycles in that order).
- **State**: `catalog` (`MusicCatalog.site()`, built with the model), the covers (each album's embedded cover as an `ImageBitmap` by album id, `cover(albumId)`; those already in memory, `musicCachedCover`, are there from the model's first frame), `rootView`, `backStack` (the Artists root first), the queue (private), `currentIndex`, `currentTime` (seconds), `isPlaying`, `isShuffling`, `repeatMode`, the overlays `showingMenu`, `showingQueue`, `queueMenuTarget` (the held item) and `toast`. Derived: `currentEntry` / `currentTrack` / `currentAlbum` / `currentArtist`, `upNext`, `canGoBack` / `canGoForward`, `isCurrent(track)`, and `hostsQueue(route)` (whether the Queue sheet opens over the stack's page at `route`: the page on top while Artists shows; the Player hosts it while it shows).
- **The queue is history, the current entry, then up next.** A fresh model queues every song, paused on the first. History is the played list's songs before the one play began on, then what played after it, shuffle included; stepping back walks it in that order.
- **Steps**: Repeat all wraps, and a one-entry queue never steps. `goTo(index)` clamps, zeroes `currentTime` and loads that track's stream, playing on if it was playing, even for the same index. Previous never restarts a track.
- **Track end** (the player's completion): Repeat one replays, and so does Repeat all on a one-entry queue; otherwise `next()` if it can; otherwise it parks paused on the last track at 0:00. It runs wherever the scene is showing, so the queue moves on under Artists, a panel or the Queue sheet.
- **Shuffle** on shuffles up next; off puts up next back in the order its entries were queued (a private queued-order list). The current entry and history never move.
- **Play**: `play(tracks, index, shuffled)` replaces the queue with fresh entries, the one at `index` current; `true` / `false` turn shuffle on / off, null keeps it (shuffling what follows); it asks for the audio focus, plays if granted, and switches to the Player. `play(target, shuffled = false)` closes the spotlight and plays an artist's or album's songs from the first with that shuffle setting (the Play and Shuffle buttons and tiles), or a song from its place in the list it was held in, keeping the shuffle setting, as tapping its row does.
- **Queue edits**: `addToQueue(target)` appends fresh entries, closes the spotlight and toasts "Added to the queue" / "Added 3 songs to the queue"; `removeFromQueue(id)` and `moveUpNext(from, to)` act on up next only.
- **Navigation**: `openArtist`, `openAlbum` push; `back()` pops (the root stays); `switchTo(view)` closes the menu outside `amInstantSwitch`, then sets `rootView` inside it, never touching the stack; `openQueue()` closes the menu and opens the sheet.
- **Streaming** (`MediaPlayer`, no extra dependency): one player per track, `USAGE_MEDIA` / `CONTENT_TYPE_MUSIC`, `setDataSource` on the site's MP3, `prepareAsync`; once prepared it seeks to `currentTime` (`SEEK_CLOSEST`) and starts if playback is on. A half-second tick (a coroutine) copies the player's position into `currentTime` while it plays, and holds it while the stream prepares, while the scrubber is held, and while a seek is on its way, so a seek's target stays on the scrubber until the stream gets there. A stream that fails (`onError`, offline) stops playback and drops the player, so the next Play streams the track again. **Seeking**: `scrub(seconds)` (the scrubber moving) moves the clock, stopping 1 s short of the track's end (a seek never ends a track; only playback reaching the end moves the queue on); `seekToClock()` (the slider's `onValueChangeFinished`: a drag's end, a tap, a TalkBack adjustment) seeks the player to the clock. `stop()` stops the tick, releases the player and abandons the focus.
- **Audio focus**: an `AudioFocusRequest` for `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN` with the music attributes, asked for on every play. Losing it for good pauses and lets it go; losing it for a moment pauses and plays on when it returns; ducking is the system's.
- **`load()`** readies the current track's stream, paused, then reads every cover not yet in memory from its album's first MP3 (`musicEmbeddedCover`), all at once, each a coroutine in the scene's scope, landing in the covers as it's read. Leaving the scene cancels what's still loading.
- **Saved** (`MusicModel.saver(scope, context)`, a `mapSaver`): the root view, the stack (as `artist:id` / `album:id`), the queue (each entry's id and song), the queued order, the index and time, shuffle and repeat, the menu, the Queue sheet and the held item. Playback and the player aren't kept: a restored scene is paused where it was, its covers back from memory on its first frame.

## Data — `MusicData.kt`

- **`MusicCatalog.site()`** is the owner's music from jmsdnns.com/music, hardcoded: every project the page lists, American Food in the present and six bands in the past — 7 artists, 13 albums, 61 songs. American Food's three EPs (Silent Artillery Of Time, Internet On The TV, Street Fight; 3 songs each), then Adventure Capitalists (Unicore, MVP, No Spoilers Allowed), Sea Graves (Congratulations), Medio (Live @ Pianos 07.11.15), Shipyards (A Dream Is A Town), First Aid Kit (One Offs, Forgot ID, Frights And Shivers) and Counterfit (On The Downside). Song titles and lengths (whole seconds) are the site's; each song's MP3 path is percent-encoded as the site links it, under `https://jmsdnns.com/`.
- **`MusicCatalog.build(albums, tracks, artistImage)`** is the one place that sorts: albums A–Z (`Collator`), grouped into artists by the album's artist (each artist's albums in title order), artists by their newest album's year, newest first (a tie A–Z), tracks in album then track order. An album's id is the slug of its title, a track's leads with its album's (`mvp-no-spoilers-allowed`), track numbers count from 1.
- **The album years set the artists' order**: American Food, Adventure Capitalists, Sea Graves, Medio, Shipyards, First Aid Kit, Counterfit. American Food's EPs are 2024; Sea Graves' 2016, Shipyards' 2008 and Counterfit's 2000 are set for the order. The years show nowhere.
- **Pictures.** An artist's photo is an image on the site with its aspect ratio (2:1, Counterfit's 1000:451), always shown whole. An album's `coverUrl` is its project page's header image, the fallback wherever the album's cover shows until it's read (and if it can't be). An album's own cover lives only in its MP3s' ID3v2 tags (`MusicCovers.kt`, below). Nothing is bundled or written to disk.
- `MusicQueueEntry(id, track)` (a fresh entry mints a UUID, so the same song can be queued twice); `MusicQueueTarget` (`Artist`, `Album`, `Track(track, context)`; `tracks(catalog)` is what Add to queue adds).
- Labels: "3:41", "1 song" / "9 songs", "11 min", "3 songs · 15 min", "3 albums · 9 songs". `MusicFabClearance` = 88 dp.

## Covers — `MusicCovers.kt`

- **`musicEmbeddedCover(url)`** (suspend): the cover in memory if this app has read it (`MusicCoverCache`, a platform `LruCache` of 64 MB by decoded size, room for every album's cover; `musicCachedCover(url)` reads it synchronously); otherwise `runInterruptible(Dispatchers.IO) { musicFetchId3Tag(url) }`, then on `Dispatchers.Default` `musicId3Cover` and an `ImageDecoder` decode at most 1024 px on the longest side (the largest art card on a phone); the result goes into the cache. Null when anything fails.
- **Fetching** (`musicFetchId3Tag`, blocking, `HttpURLConnection`, no caches, 15 s / 30 s timeouts, `User-Agent` `AndroidMtrls/0.1 (Android demo)`): a request with `Range: bytes=0-9`, whose 10 bytes give the tag's length (`musicId3TagLength`: "ID3", version 2–4, a revision other than 0xFF, the syncsafe size, plus the 10-byte footer when v2.4 flags one); then `Range: bytes=0-<length − 1>` for the whole tag, header included. A 200 (the server ignoring ranges) is a whole-file stream: the reader takes the tag's bytes from that first response and disconnects, never reading past the tag. Tags over 16 MB are left unread; anything but 200 or 206 gives null. The site (S3 behind CloudFront) serves ranges; American Food's tags run 5–8 MB (2400 px PNG covers), the rest 30–200 KB, and Sea Graves' 2.8 MB.
- **Parsing** (`musicId3Cover(tag)`, pure, over the whole tag): the attached picture whose type is 3 (front cover), else the first attached picture. v2.2: `PIC` frames, 6-byte headers (3-letter id, 24-bit size), a 3-letter image format; a tag flagged compressed is skipped. v2.3: `APIC` frames, 10-byte headers, plain 32-bit sizes; compressed or encrypted frames skipped, a group byte passed over. v2.4: `APIC` frames with syncsafe sizes (read plain when a size byte has its top bit set); compressed or encrypted frames skipped, the group byte and the data length indicator passed over, and a frame flagged unsynchronised, or every frame of a tag flagged so, resynchronised. In v2.2 and v2.3 the tag's unsynchronisation flag covers the whole tag, resynchronised (`musicId3Resync`: each 0xFF 0x00 back to 0xFF) before the frames are read; an extended header (v2.3: its size plus 4; v2.4: its syncsafe size) is skipped. A picture frame is skipped past its text encoding, its MIME type (zero-ended) or format, the picture type, and the description (ended by one zero byte in ISO-8859-1 and UTF-8, two on a two-byte boundary in UTF-16); a link picture ("-->") is passed over. Frames end at padding (bytes that aren't a frame id of capital letters and digits) or at a frame that runs past the tag.
- On the site the tags are v2.2 (most), v2.3 (First Aid Kit) and v2.4 (Shipyards), none unsynchronised, their pictures typed "other": every album's first MP3 gives its cover.

## Reaching each screen

From the landing view (Artists): tap American Food → the artist page; tap Internet On The TV → the album page; tap Wanna Be A Cat → the Player; tap the + ("Actions") → the remote over the Player; Queue → the Queue sheet. On the album page, press and hold a track → the spotlight with Play and Add to queue. The same with Sea Graves → Congratulations → Movement.
