# Shop scene

**Ferry Line**, a made-up coffee roaster in Red Hook: its catalog and label art, quick add, the bag, checkout and its discard guard, and order tracking. Its entry is `shopScene` ("Shop", "A coffee shop with a bag, checkout, and orders", `ic_storefront`), fifth in `ShowcasePages.entries`. Three root views (Shop, Bag, Orders) swap from the remote; each is a Navigation 3 back stack whose panels (a product, an order, the bag over the shop) push over it. The shared scene rules are in `DemoScenes.md`.

## The root — `ShopScene()`

- `rememberCoroutineScope()`, then the model: `rememberSaveable(saver = saver) { ShopModel(scope) }`, its saver `remember(scope) { ShopModel.saver(scope) }`. A `DisposableEffect` cancels pending work as the scene leaves; a `LaunchedEffect` runs `load()`. `showsRemote` is its own saved flag.
- **`AMScene`'s roots form on the beans**: one `AMBackdrop(art = { ShopBackdrop() }, Modifier.fillMaxSize())` holds the whole scene, an `AMScene(current = currentView, landing = Shop, onCurrentChange, floatingActions, overlays = { ShopOverlays(model) }, backStack = { model.stack(it) }, containerColor = Color.Transparent) { view -> ShopRootStack(model, view) }`, its remote in `floatingActions` (`AMFloatingActions`, "Actions"). The scene lies transparent on the beans, so the roots' frosted cards and the frosted sheets (which find their art through the presenting composition) blur the same backdrop. The overlays sit beside the roots, outside every one, so Place order switching to Orders leaves the checkout sheet sliding away.
- **`ShopRootStack(model, view)`**: a `NavDisplay` over `model.stack(view)`, `onBack` popping it; entries by route: `Root(Shop)` → `ShopHome`, `Root(Bag)` → `ShopBag(onBack = null)`, `Root(Orders)` → `ShopOrders`, `Product(id)` → `ShopProductPanel`, `Order(number)` → `ShopOrderPanel`, `Bag` → `ShopBag(onBack = pop)`. Root views lie transparent over the scene's backdrop; each panel brings its own `AMBackdrop` of the beans, so it covers the root it pushes over.
- **Back**: the spotlight or the remote, then a sheet (the checkout asking first while it holds changes), then the root's panels, then Bag or Orders swap to Shop (the roots form's step back to the landing view), then out to the catalog. The loading overlay holds back while an order is placed.
- **Overlays** (`ShopOverlays`): the "Search" and "Checkout" `AMSheet`s (full height, `AMSheetSurface.Frosted`; what they hold is solid), the quick-add `AMSpotlight` (item form, up while `showsQuickAdd`), `AMLoadingOverlay(isPlacingOrder, "Placing order…")` (composed after the checkout, so it opens over it) and `AMToast(toast)`.
- **`shopFillsCheckout`** debug extra (`--es shopFillsCheckout yes`; debug builds only): the checkout sheet reads `LaunchOptions.shopFillsCheckout` (`Demo.md`) and opens filled in with the sample address.

### Shared pieces (`ShopViews.kt`)

- **`ShopBackdrop()`**: roasted coffee beans (`commonsPhoto("Roasted_coffee_beans.jpg")`, public domain) through `Modifier.amPageBackdrop(blurRadius = 3.5 dp light / 17 dp dark, lightWash = 0.55, darkWash = 0.4)`, sharp enough to read as beans; dark blurs more, since its lighter wash would leave the highlights hard. Section headers and footers on it take the over-art look (`onSurface` with the text shadow), a panel's back arrow and the light/dark chip are solid, and the bars take the frosted fill once scrolled, all from the library.
- **`ShopRootPage(title, actions, content)`**: a root view's `Scaffold` on a transparent container under `AMPageHeader(title, actions, exitUntilCollapsedScrollBehavior)`; the content gets the scaffold's padding.
- **`ShopLabelArt(product, style)`**: a product's typographic label, the same everywhere: its `color` with `ink` type. `ShopLabelStyle.Thumb` (a 40 dp square in `shapes.small` with the label's first letter), `Tile` (a square grid label in `shapes.small`: "FERRY LINE" over the label title, a line per word up to two, and its footer, padded `spacing.md`), `Hero` (the panel's 3:2 label in `shapes.large`, padded `spacing.xl`), `Banner(kicker, blurb)` (a carousel page filling its frame in `shapes.large`: the kicker in capitals over the label title, the blurb and the price label, padded `spacing.lg`). Packaging, not UI text: every size and tracking is set in dp (`Dp.toSp()`), so it doesn't follow the font scale; label titles step down to 60 % to fit (`TextAutoSize.StepBased`); TalkBack skips it (`clearAndSetSemantics`), since the card or row around it names the product.
- **`ShopProductCard(product, modifier, interaction, loading)`**: a frosted `amCard` padded `spacing.sm`: the `Tile` label over the name (`titleSmall`, two lines reserved) and the price label (`bodyMedium`, tabular, `Frosted.secondaryText`). `interaction` goes after the card, so the ripple keeps its shape. Loading, the same card holds a shimmering square (`AMSkeleton.Rect`) over `AMSkeleton.Text(name, style, minLines = 2, maxLines = 2)` and `AMSkeleton.Text(priceLabel, style, maxLines = 1)`, bars as wide as the real words in the real text's size, so it's the size it will be, and says nothing to TalkBack.
- **`ShopTotalsRows(subtotal, shipping)`**: Subtotal and Shipping ("Free" at 0) as `AMLabeledContent` rows with tabular figures, one inset `AMDivider`, then Total in semibold `onSurface`. The Bag, Checkout and the order panel share it.
- **`ShopFabClearance`** = 88 dp.

## The data — `ShopData.kt`

- **13 products**, one category each, in grid order: ten coffees (five Filter, three Espresso, two Decaf; Origin, Process and Roast as details, tasting notes as notes, a brewing guide), then three gear (Ceramic Dripper, Hand Grinder, Gooseneck Kettle; their own details, features as notes, a how-to guide). Prices are cents: a coffee's 250 g bag, ×1.8 for 500 g and ×3.3 for 1 kg, rounded to whole dollars; gear ignores the size. `priceLabel` is "From $21.00" or "$95.00"; `searchLine` "Light roast · From $21.00" or "Gear · $95.00". Units use a non-breaking space ("250 g").
- **Featured**: Kenya Gatomboya ("Just landed"), Night Ferry ("House espresso"), the Hand Grinder ("Back in stock").
- **Orders**: `ShopOrder(number, placed, lines, delivery, shipTo)`. Its status comes from its step dates and now: `steps(now)` dates Roasted the next day at 7:30, Shipped two days after at 15:15, Delivered five days after at 13:20 (Standard) or three days after at 11:05 (Express); a step is done once its date has passed, and `status(now)` is the last done one. Nothing ticks, so a new order stays Ordered. Tags: Ordered neutral, Roasted warning, Shipped info, Delivered success.
- **Samples relative to now** (`ShopSamples.make`): #1043 three days ago (Standard, Night Ferry 1 kg and the dripper, $87.00, shipped), #1041 nine days ago (Express, two 250 g Ethiopia Hambela, $42.00 + $15.00, delivered), #1038 23 days ago ($59.00, delivered). All ship to Ada Lovelace, 214 Van Brunt St, Brooklyn, NY 11231.
- **Delivery**: Standard $6.00, free from a $50.00 subtotal ("Arrives in 3–5 days. Free over $50."); Express $15.00 ("Arrives in 1–2 days.").

## The model — `ShopModel`

A plain `@Stable` class of Compose state, built on the scene's coroutine scope.

- **Navigation**: `currentView: ShopView` (`Shop`, `Bag`, `Orders`) and one `SnapshotStateList<ShopRoute>` per view (`stack(view)`), its root first. `ShopRoute` is `Root(view)`, `Product(id)`, `Order(number)` or `Bag`, saved as text (`encode` / `decode`). `push` skips a route already on the stack (a double tap); `pop(view)` never takes the root. `openProduct`, `openOrder`, `openBagPanel` push on the showing view.
- **Catalog**: `filter` (`ShopFilter`), `featuredIndex`, `shownProducts`; `isLoading` until `load()` waits a second, once.
- **Bag**: `add(id, size, grind, quantity)` drops the size and grind for gear, merges into the line with the same product, size and grind (up to 99), and toasts "Added Kenya Gatomboya, 500 g" (" × 2" past one). `setQuantity` keeps 1…99 and `remove` drops a line, neither toasting. `bagCount` (quantities summed), `bagSubtotal`.
- **Quick add**: `showQuickAdd(id)` sets `quickAddId` (never cleared, so the spotlight's card fades out intact) and `showsQuickAdd`; `quickAdd(id, size)` closes it and adds one bag, Whole bean for coffee.
- **Search**: `beginSearch()` empties `searchQuery` (a `TextFieldState`) and opens the sheet; `searchResults` is every product while the query is blank, else those whose name, label, category, detail values or notes contain it; `openSearchResult(id)` closes the sheet and leaves the showing view's stack as its root and that product.
- **Checkout**: `beginCheckout()` copies the bag into `checkoutLines`, which the sheet's totals read, so they hold while the sheet slides away; `placeOrder(shipTo, delivery)` (ignored while placing) waits 1.5 s behind "Placing order…", then numbers the order one past the highest (#1044 first), puts it first, empties the bag, closes the sheet outside `amInstantSwitch` (so its slide plays), toasts "Order #1044 placed", and inside `amInstantSwitch` pops a bag panel the order came from, switches to Orders and leaves its stack as its root and the new order.
- **Cancellation**: the order in flight is a `Job`; `cancelPendingWork()` cancels it and clears `isPlacingOrder`. It runs from the Exit tile and when the scene leaves the composition.
- **Saved** (`ShopModel.saver`, a `mapSaver`): the current view and every stack, the filter and featured page, whether the shop has landed, the bag and the orders placed here (as `Bundle`s), quick add's product and whether it's up, the query and whether Search is up, the checkout's lines and whether it's up. The samples are made again, relative to now. Nothing in flight is kept.

One `LazyVerticalGrid` of two columns, `spacing.md` both ways, running edge to edge with each column keeping its own gutter: "This week" (`AMSectionHeader`) over a full-width `AMCarousel` of `Banner` labels, `ShopFeatureHeight` (180 dp) tall, each page one button ("Just landed, Kenya Gatomboya, From $23.00") that pushes its product, over a centered `AMPageIndicator`; a single-choice `AMChipGroup` of `ShopFilter`; then a `ShopProductCard` per shown product. - **Quick add** (`ShopQuickAddPreview`, `ShopQuickAddTiles`): the spotlight raises the held card at `ShopPreviewCardWidth` (220 dp), read as one element (its frosted card nests flat in the spotlight's solid one), over one `AMActionGridRow`: coffee gets "250 g", "500 g", "1 kg" (`ic_add_shopping_cart`, read "Add 500 g, $41.00"), each adding one Whole bean bag; gear gets "Add to bag" and "Details", which closes it and pushes the product.
- **Product** (`ShopProductPanel`): an `AMPanel` titled with the name on a transparent container inside its own beans `AMBackdrop`, a column `spacing.xl` apart: the `Hero` label in a solid `amCard` over the name (`titleLarge`) and the price line (`titleMedium`, tabular: "$41.00 · 500 g" following the size, the price alone for gear); "Details" (`AMSection`, frosted) with the facts in two columns (labels as wide as the widest, in `Frosted.secondaryText`) over the notes as neutral `AMTag`s in an `AMFlowLayout`, read as one element ("Tasting notes: …", gear "Features: …"); for coffee, "Grind" over a full-width `AMSegmentedControl` and "Size" over a single-choice `AMChipGroup`; a frosted card holding the `AMStepper` (1…9, named "Quantity", its label "Qty 2" hidden from TalkBack) over full-width "Add to bag", which adds and leaves the panel as it is; then the guide as a frosted `AMDisclosureGroup` of numbered steps. - **Search** (`ShopSearchSheet`): `AMSearchField` ("Search coffee and gear", focused as the sheet opens) in a card padded `spacing.lg`, over a solid card of `AMListRow`s (the `Thumb` label, the name, `searchLine`) that hugs its rows and scrolls inside past the room left, or "No matches" (`AMEmptyState`, "Nothing matches “q”. Dates follow the phone's locale and 12- or 24-hour clock (`getBestDateTimePattern`).

## The remote

Search (`ic_search`), Bag (`ic_shopping_bag`) over Shop (`ic_storefront`), Orders (`ic_package_2`), Exit (`ic_logout`), the same wherever the scene stands; nothing greys. Bag, Shop and Orders are view tiles: the showing one is `active`; a tap closes the menu, then sets `currentView` inside `amInstantSwitch`, each view coming back with its panels as it was left. Search closes the menu and opens the Search sheet. Exit closes the menu, cancels pending work and calls `LocalCatalogNavigator.current.back()`.

## Reaching each screen

From the landing view (Shop): the remote ("Actions") opens the menu. Hold a grid card → quick add (a size tile adds a bag and toasts). Tap a card or a carousel page → the product panel. The remote's Search → the Search sheet; a result → its product. Add something, then the bag button ("Bag") → the bag as a panel, or the remote's Bag → the Bag root. Checkout → the Checkout sheet (`--es shopFillsCheckout yes` fills it in); Place order → "Placing order…", then Orders with the new order open. The remote's Orders → Orders; a row ("Order #1043") → its tracking.
