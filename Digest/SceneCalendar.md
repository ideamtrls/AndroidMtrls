# Calendar scene

A calendar: one month at a time with the selected day's agenda under it, events and their repeats, the now marker, the day peek on a hold, the event form, and a months view, over a picture of the month's weather with frosted cards. Its entry is `calendarScene` ("Calendar", "A month calendar with a day's events", `ic_calendar_month`), fourth in `ShowcasePages.entries`. Nothing pushes: the month view and the months view are the two roots of `AMScene`'s roots form, which the remote swaps. The shared scene rules are in `DemoScenes.md`.

## Where things live

`demo/src/main/kotlin/com/ideamtrls/androidmtrls/demo/catalog/scenes/calendar/`, one file per surface:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `CalendarScene.kt` | `CalendarSceneEntry`, `CalendarFabClearance`, `CalendarScene()` (the root); private `CalendarRemote`, `CalendarOverlays` |
| `CalendarModel.kt` | `CalendarView`, `CalendarModel` and its `Saver`, `CalendarEventTimes`, `CalendarDraft` |
| `CalendarData.kt` | `CalendarEventCalendar`, `CalendarEventRepeat`, `CalendarEventAlert`, `CalendarEvent` (and `tint`), `CalendarAgendaEntry`, `calendarAgenda`, `calendarCompactAgenda`, `CalendarDates`, `CalendarFormats` and `rememberCalendarFormats`, `rememberCalendarNow`, `calendarMonthArt`, `calendarSampleEvents` (private `CalendarSamples`) |
| `CalendarMonthView.kt` | `CalendarMonthView` (the landing view), `CalendarNothingScheduled`, `CalendarAgendaTimeline`; private `CalendarDayAgenda`, `CalendarNowLabel`, `CalendarMoreLabel` |
| `CalendarDayPeek.kt` | `CalendarDayPeek`; private `CalendarDayPeekAgenda` |
| `CalendarMonthsView.kt` | `CalendarMonthsView`; private `CalendarMonthRow` |

Glyphs it adds to the demo's `res/drawable/`: `ic_calendar_month` (the entry, the Month tile), `ic_calendar_add_on` (Add event), `ic_event_available` (Open day). It also uses `ic_radio_button_checked` (Today), `ic_calendar_clock` (Years), `ic_logout` (Exit), `ic_calendar_today` (the empty day), `ic_check` (the month on show), and `ic_light_mode` / `ic_dark_mode` through `SceneAppearanceButton`.

## The root — `CalendarScene()`

- The model: `rememberSaveable(saver = CalendarModel.Saver) { CalendarModel() }`; `showsRemote` is its own saved flag.
- **The backdrop.** An `AMBackdrop` fills the scene; its art is `Modifier.amPageBackdrop(calendarMonthArt(month))`, the month on show while the month view is current and this month while the months view is. The art fades to a new month's picture (Today, Open day, a save that moves the month) and snaps when the change comes inside `amInstantSwitch` (a root swap, a month picked). Everything sits inside it, so the frosted cards and the frosted sheet blur the picture, the section headers take their over-art look and the light/dark chip is solid.
- **The scene.** `AMScene`'s roots form over `CalendarView` (`Month`, the landing view, and `Months`), `containerColor = Color.Transparent`, the remote `AMFloatingActions(contentDescription = "Actions")` in its slot. Each root is `CalendarMonthView` or `CalendarMonthsView`; `CalendarOverlays` fills the scene's `overlays` slot, outside both roots, so a swap of view never disposes an open overlay, and the overlays report to the scene and their toasts use its host. Back: an alert, the spotlight or the remote, the sheet, then the months view swaps back to the month view, then the scene leaves.
- **Overlays** (`CalendarOverlays`): `CalendarEventSheet`; the delete `AMAlert`, composed after the sheet so it opens over it ("Delete event?" / "“Title” will be removed from your calendar.", Cancel and a destructive Delete; the title is the stored event's); `CalendarDayPeek`; `AMToast`. Toasts: "Event added", "Event updated", "Event deleted".
- **`CalendarFabClearance`** = 88 dp: the button's 56 dp with `spacing.lg` under and over it.

## The data — `CalendarData.kt`

- **`CalendarEventCalendar`**: Home (`AMSemantic.Success.color`), Work (`AMSemantic.Info.color`, the accent), Family (purple, `#AF52DE` light and `#BF5AF2` dark), each with a `title`. **`CalendarEventRepeat`**: Never, Every day, Every week, Every month. **`CalendarEventAlert`**: None, At time of event, 5 minutes before, 15 minutes before, 1 hour before.
- **`CalendarEvent`** (`@Immutable` data class on `java.time`): `title`, `start` and `end` (`LocalDateTime`), `calendar`, `id` (a UUID string), `isAllDay`, `location`, `colorOverride` (one event's own color; the sample anniversary's pink), `notes`, `repeatRule`, `alert`. `tint` is the override or the calendar's color. `firstDay` is the start's day; `lastDay` the day of the moment before the end, never before `firstDay`, so an end exactly at midnight stays on the day before (timed or all-day alike). **Repeats**: `occurrence(day)` returns the event or a copy (same `id`) moved by whole days, weeks or months from its own start to the repeat that touches `day`, else null; repeats never end and never come before the first occurrence; a monthly repeat skips a month without its day number (the 31st in June); the latest start wins an overlap. `occursOn(day)` is `occurrence(day) != null`. `toBundle` / `fromBundle` save it.
- **The agenda.** `CalendarAgendaEntry`: `Event`, `Now(time)`, `More(count)`, each with a `key`. `calendarAgenda(day, events, now)`: the day's occurrences, all-day first, then timed by start (then end, then title); on `now`'s day with a timed event, `Now` sits after the events that have started (`start <= now`) and before the rest; no timed events, no `Now`. `calendarCompactAgenda(…, limit)` cuts it just before event `limit + 1` and closes it with `More(n)`; `Now` stays when it comes before the cut.
- **`CalendarFormats`** (`rememberCalendarFormats()`, per locale and 12- or 24-hour setting): `monthTitle` ("November 2026", best pattern `MMMMy`), `monthName` (standalone full name), `dayHeading` ("Today", else `EEEEMMMd`, "Tuesday, Nov 10"), `time` (`hm` / `Hm`, "9:41 AM"), `timeLabel` ("All day"; a same-day event through `DateUtils.formatDateRange` with `FORMAT_SHOW_TIME`, "9:00 – 9:45 AM"; an end on a later day, midnight included, as its two times joined by a thin-spaced en dash, "9:30 PM – 2:00 AM"), `agendaDetail` ("9:00 – 9:45 AM · Room 4B", the times alone without a location).
- **`rememberCalendarNow()`**: the time now, read through `LaunchOptions.clock()` and refreshed at each minute's start (`produceState`), so the now marker and "Today" follow the clock across midnight; a `demoTime` launch pins the time of day (the date stays today's).
- **`calendarMonthArt(month)`**: one Wikimedia Commons picture per month of the year (`commonsPhoto`), January's winter beech forest through December's Karlštejn in winter.
- **`calendarSampleEvents(today)`**: 28 events, weekday-aware. Today is full either way: the all-day "Dad's birthday" plus seven timed events (weekdays: a 7:00 run, stand-up, design review, lunch with Priya, a soccer pickup, the birthday dinner, a 21:30 call with the Tokyo team; weekends: an 8:00 run, the farmers market, brunch, Leo's soccer game, a bike ride, the dinner, a 21:30 movie night). The next six days take one plan each, in order, from five workday plans (two events each) for weekdays and two weekend plans (an all-day day trip; a swim lesson and a barbecue) for Saturdays and Sundays, always 11 events. Nine more scatter across two months either side (two all-day birthdays, the pink anniversary dinner), work and weekday events moved to the nearest weekday (Saturday to Friday, Sunday to Monday). None repeat.

## The model — `CalendarModel`

A plain `@Stable` class of Compose state, remembered at the root. No stand-in requests, so nothing to cancel.

- **State**: `events` (a snapshot list, repeats stored once as their series), `currentView`, `displayedMonth` (`YearMonth`, this month at first), `selectedDay` (today at first), `peekDay`, `showsEventSheet`, `draft` (kept after the form closes, so the sheet slides away intact), `confirmsDelete`, `toast`. Derived: `isViewingToday` (this month and today selected), `draftStoredTitle`.
- **Actions**: `selectDay`; `showToday`; `pickMonth(month)` (inside `amInstantSwitch`: the month, its 1st selected, the month view); `newEvent(day)`; `editEvent(occurrence)` (the stored series by `id`, its own dates; Save and Delete apply to the whole series); `closeEventSheet`; `save()` (over the matching `id`, "Event updated", or appended, "Event added"; an event that doesn't fall on the selected day takes the selection and the month to its first day; closes the form); `confirmDelete`; `delete()` (removes the series, closes the form, "Event deleted"); `addEvent(day)` and `openDay(day)` for the peek.
- **Saved** (`CalendarModel.Saver`, a `mapSaver`): the events (a `Bundle` each), the view, month and day, the peek's day, whether the form is up and its draft, and the delete confirmation.
- **`CalendarDraft`**, the form's working copy: `id` (the stored event's, or a fresh one) and `isNew` (the sheet's title, Delete's absence); `TextFieldState`s `title`, `location`, `notes`; `calendar`, `repeatRule`, `alert`; and `start`, `end`, `isAllDay`, whose setters keep the rules. Moving Starts carries Ends along, keeping the length (exactly while timed, in whole days while all-day). Ends never goes before Starts. All-day on keeps the times of day (`CalendarEventTimes`: start and end time, and `endDayOffset`, 1 when the event ends exactly at midnight) and shows the days the event touches, held as midnights; All-day off puts the kept times back on those days (the event's own, or 9:00 to 10:00 for a new event or an all-day event that never had times), an hour long if that would end before it starts. `canSave` waits for a non-blank title. `saved` trims the title and location, puts an all-day event's kept times back (a day later when the end would land on the midnight opening the last day shown) and never ends before it starts. `new(day)` is 9:00 to 10:00 on Home; `editing(event)` shows an all-day event's days, keeping the times it was saved with (the midnight-to-midnight samples get 9:00 to 10:00). `toBundle` / `fromBundle` save it.

## The surfaces

- **Month view** (`CalendarMonthView`, the landing view): a `Scaffold` on a transparent container under `AMPageHeader("November 2026", actions = SceneAppearanceButton())`, collapsing (`exitUntilCollapsedScrollBehavior`). A scrolling column inset by the page gutter, `spacing.sm` at the top, blocks `spacing.xl` apart, ending `CalendarFabClearance` above the navigation bar: `AMCalendar` (the displayed month, the selected day, `markers` counting the events that occur each day, repeats included; `onHold` sets `peekDay`) in a frosted card; then the day's agenda. A tap on a day selects it; a hold peeks at it without selecting it.
- **The day's agenda** (`CalendarDayAgenda`): the heading ("Today" or "Tuesday, Nov 10") over `CalendarAgendaTimeline` in `AMSection(…, style = Card, fill = Frosted, elevation = None, inset = false)`; a clear day shows `AMSectionHeader` over `CalendarNothingScheduled` (`AMEmptyState`, `ic_calendar_today`, "Nothing scheduled" / "No events on this day.", padded `spacing.lg`) on the backdrop, never on a card.
- **`CalendarAgendaTimeline(entries, modifier, onSelectEvent)`**: an `AMTimeline` keyed by entry. An event is `AMTimelineDot(color = tint)` beside `AMTimelineLabel(title, subtitle = agendaDetail, tabularFigures = true)` (even digits, so the times line up from event to event), a clickable (`Role.Button`, ripple clipped to `shapes.small`) that opens the event's series in the form; with no `onSelectEvent` (the peek) it's read-only, merged into one element. The now entry is a destructive dot beside `CalendarNowLabel`: "Now · 2:41 PM" (`bodySmall`, semibold, tabular digits, `AMSemantic.Destructive`) and a destructive rule two hairlines thick to the end, read as "Now, 2:41 PM". A more entry is the default quiet dot beside `CalendarMoreLabel`, "+2 more" in the same caption in `onSurfaceVariant`, read as "2 more". Both captions carry `amTimelineFirstLine`, so the dot centers on them. Events carry no glyph.
- **Day peek** (`CalendarDayPeek`): `AMSpotlight(peekDay, title = dayHeading, elevation = AMElevation.None)`, solid, flat (no shadow, like the scene's cards) and low on the screen: `calendarCompactAgenda(…, limit = 4)` on a read-only `CalendarAgendaTimeline`, or `CalendarNothingScheduled`, padded `spacing.lg`; under it one `AMActionGridRow`, Add event (`ic_calendar_add_on`: closes the peek, opens the form on the held day; Save then moves the selection there) and Open day (`ic_event_available`: shows the held day's month with the day selected, closes the peek). Back and a tap outside close it. On TalkBack the hold is each day's long-press action.
- **Months view** (`CalendarMonthsView`): headerless; its year headers are its only titling. A full-screen `LazyColumn` with `Modifier.amStatusBarEdge()` (rows scrolling up under the status bar fade out there, the month's picture showing behind the clock), the status bar's height at the top of its `contentPadding`, the page gutter at the sides, `CalendarFabClearance` above the navigation bar at the end: years three back through three forward, each `AMSection("2026", style = Rows, fill = Frosted, inset = false)` with `spacing.xl` above it (part of the item, so a year opened at the top rests its gap below the status bar), holding twelve `AMListRow`s (`CalendarMonthRow`: the month's name, and `ic_check` in `onSurface` on the month on show, each row's `selected` semantics true there) with an `AMDivider(inset = spacing.lg)` between each two. A tap calls `pickMonth`. It first opens at the displayed month's year (`rememberLazyListState(initialFirstVisibleItemIndex)`), then where it was left.
- **Event form** (`CalendarEventSheet`): `AMSheet(title = "New event" / "Edit event", surface = Frosted)`, full height, no discard guard. `CalendarEventForm` fills it: a scrolling column padded `spacing.lg`, sections `spacing.xl` apart, all flat (`elevation = None`, `inset = false`), solid on the glass:
  - **Details** (`Card`): `AMTextField` "Title" (sentences, Next; `CaretAtEnd` autofocus on a new event) and "Location" (words, Done), `spacing.md` apart, no divider.
  - **Time** (`Rows`): `AMToggle` "All-day", `AMDatePicker` "Starts" and "Ends" (`DateAndTime`, or `Date` while all-day; Ends' `dateRange` starts at Starts' day), bare.
  - **Repeat and alert** (`Rows`): `AMMenuPicker`s "Repeat" and "Alert".
  - **Calendar** (`Rows`): `AMMenuPicker` "Calendar" (Home, Work, Family); the event's color follows it unless the event has its own.
  - **Notes** (`Card`): `AMTextEditor` "Notes", placeholder "Directions, links, what to bring".
  - Under the scrolling form, padded `spacing.lg` (`spacing.sm` above): `AMFormActions(elevation = None)`, Save alone for a new event, Delete (destructive) and Save for an edit; Save is disabled until there's a title; Delete asks first.

## The remote

`[Add event, Today]` over `[Month, Years, Exit]`. Add event (`ic_calendar_add_on`) closes the menu and opens the form on the selected day. Today (`ic_radio_button_checked`) sets the month view to this month with today selected, leaving the current view as it is, and closes the menu; it's disabled while that's already set. Month (`ic_calendar_month`) and Years (`ic_calendar_clock`) are view tiles: the current one is `active`; a tap closes the menu, then sets `currentView` inside `amInstantSwitch`. Exit (`ic_logout`) closes the menu and calls `LocalCatalogNavigator.current.back()`.

## Reaching each screen

From the landing view (the month view, today selected): scroll → the agenda under the month. Hold a day → its peek; Add event → the new-event form; Open day → that day selected. The remote ("Actions") → its menu; Add event → the new-event form (Starts or Ends → the date in the spotlight; Repeat, Alert or Calendar → its options); Years → the months view, where a month → the month view on that month. Tap an event in the agenda → the edit-event form; Delete → the delete confirmation. The light/dark chip in the month view's header flips the appearance.
